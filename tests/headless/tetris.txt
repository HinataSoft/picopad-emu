# TETRIS determinism script: title (pause screen of the new game), start,
# a few moves and rotations, drop, more moves; ~10 s of emulated time.
# Keys are held 100 ms with gaps so the SDK debounce and the game polling see them.
500   shot title
1000  hold X 100
1500  hold LEFT 100
1800  hold LEFT 100
2100  hold UP 100
2500  hold RIGHT 100
3000  hold DOWN 100
4500  shot game1
5000  hold RIGHT 100
5300  hold RIGHT 100
5600  hold UP 100
6000  hold LEFT 100
6500  hold DOWN 100
8000  hold LEFT 100
8300  hold UP 100
8700  hold DOWN 100
10000 shot game2
10500 quit
